The calculation, in plain sightPer visit = the higher of (area × base rate × service multiplier) or the minimum.
A weekly option: monthly midpoint = per visit × visits per week × 52 ÷ 12.
Fortnightly uses visits per year ÷ 12. Monthly uses visits per month. A one-off job is the visit charge on its own.
Range = that figure ± the selected percentage, rounded outwards to whole pounds. At ± 0% the figure is the midpoint itself, with no band. This range is a demo setting, not a confidence interval.
