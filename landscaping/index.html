Edit the fictional rate assumptions

Demo values, not a quote. A live firm would use its own approved rates and would not let a visitor change them. Job choices, such as lawn size and whether waste is taken away, stay available either way.

The calculation, in plain sightLawn: per visit is the higher of (area × £ / m²) or the minimum, to the nearest penny. Weekly visits = season weeks. Fortnightly visits = season weeks ÷ 2. Monthly visits = season months. Season total = per visit × visits. Monthly average = season total ÷ season months. A one-off is the visit charge alone.
Hedge: length × the height-band £ / m, or the minimum, then the optional waste fee.
Tidy: hours × the hourly rate, or the minimum, then the optional waste fee.
Range = that season total, monthly average or job total ± the selected percentage, rounded outwards to whole pounds. At ± 0% the figure itself is shown, with no band. This range is a demo setting, not a confidence interval.
